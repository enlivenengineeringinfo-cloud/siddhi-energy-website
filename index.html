MOUNTING STRUCTURES

One roof. Two strong ways to build.

We offer a welded box-pipe frame and a C-channel nut-and-bolt system. Both can be excellent choices; the right one depends on your roof, design and future plans.

Box-pipe welding structureA tailored, fixed rooftop frame

BOX PIPE • WELDED

Built as one solid frame.

Box-pipe members are welded into a fixed structure, allowing the installation team to tailor the frame to the roof layout and panel arrangement.

  • ConnectionMembers are permanently welded together on site.
  • Best fitCustom rooftop layouts and installations intended to stay in one configuration.
  • Look & feelClean, integrated steel-frame appearance.
  • Keep in mindRelocating or changing a welded layout later can require cutting and re-welding.
Ask about welded structure ↗

C-CHANNEL • NUT & BOLT

Modular, precise and serviceable.

C-channel members are connected using engineered plates, nuts and bolts. The modular construction makes individual sections easier to access or adapt.

  • ConnectionComponents join through bolted connections and hardware.
  • Best fitProjects where future adjustments, extension or part replacement may be useful.
  • Look & feelOrganised, component-based finish with visible connection detailing.
  • Keep in mindAccurate hardware installation and periodic inspection are important.
Ask about nut & bolt ↗
